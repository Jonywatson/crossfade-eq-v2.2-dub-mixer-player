Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Crossfade Player ("the app") is a music player that runs entirely in your browser or on your device. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

Short version

The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal data, listening history, or audio files. Everything happens locally on your own device.

Your music files

When you choose songs using the "+ Songs" or "+ Folder" buttons, your browser's native file picker opens and you select files from your own device. Those files are read and played directly in your browser's audio engine. They are never uploaded, copied to a server, or made accessible to anyone else — the app has no server to upload them to in the first place. Song metadata (title, artist, album, artwork) is read from the files themselves, locally, using an in-browser tag-reading library, and is never sent anywhere.

What the app stores on your device

The app uses your browser's offline cache (a standard web feature called a Service Worker) to save its own code and interface files, so it can load and work without an internet connection after your first visit. This cache only ever contains the app's own files — never your music, your file names, or anything else about you.

The app does not use cookies, does not use browser local storage or IndexedDB, and does not maintain any login, account, or user profile of any kind.

Network activity

The only network request the app makes is to load one third-party JavaScript library (jsmediatags, used to read song tags) from a public code delivery network (jsDelivr) the first time you open the app. Like any resource loaded from the web, that request is visible to jsDelivr the same way any website visit is visible to the server hosting it. No information about your music, your usage, or your identity is included in that request.

Permissions

Depending on your device, the app may request to keep your screen awake while music is playing (so playback doesn't pause when your screen locks) and may show playback controls on your lock screen or notification shade. Both are standard browser media features, used only to control playback, and involve no data collection.

Analytics and advertising

The app contains no analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party data collection of any kind.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the developer via the GitHub repository.
